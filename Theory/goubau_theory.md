# Goubau Line Transmission Line

## Goubau line Origin
In 1950, G. Goubau has published his latest work on the topic of new transmission line, whcih is later caller as G-Line or Goubau line under the title "Surface Waves and Their Application to Transmission Lines". This new transmission line is a continuous work from the previous work that has been done by A.Sommerfeld which Sommerfeld proposed in 1899. Both Sommerfeld, and Goubau's works are done on cylindrical surface wave. The sommerfeld's structure later called as "Sommerfeld cylindrical(axial) wave" by Shelknoff. 
Both of the works are similar and with recent evolution on the field of study related to the optical fiber (optical waveguide), and evolution of the surface wave as topic the difference and definition of the terminology get burrured out due to the misused among the scholar. However there was and is a significant difference between the two proposed transmission line system.
